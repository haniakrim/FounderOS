# Billing policy

This app has no billing of its own. It takes no payments and holds no card data.

This is the rule for replica-launch and for any future paid plan built on it:

- **Monthly by default.** The default plan is billed monthly. Annual is an explicit choice, never the default.
- **No trial that converts to annual.** A trial, if offered, ends or converts to monthly only, and says so before it starts.
- **Reminder before every renewal.** An email goes out before each renewal, with the amount and the date.
- **One-click cancel in Settings.** Cancelling is a single action in Settings. No call, no form, no retention maze.
- **Pro-rated refunds on request.** Ask a human (see `MATCHCO_SUPPORT_EMAIL`) and the unused part of the period is refunded.
